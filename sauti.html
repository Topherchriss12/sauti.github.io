About us

Radio, made simple.

Running a radio station no longer requires a complex studio setup, expensive hardware, a team of technical specialists and a staff. Sauti brings the essential tools into a single, intuitive web platform so your station operates reliably, efficiently, and with zero friction.

You bring the music and the voice. We keep the station running, day and night.

Sauti is a platform built to give more people a space to be heard, connect, inspire, and lead conversations that matter.

Simple first

If you can upload a WhatsApp photo, you can run a radio station. Sauti is designed to be easy to use, even for beginners.

Always on air

Your station never sleeps. Sauti automatically fills the gaps between your shows with music and jingles, while keeping your listeners tuned in.

Yours, always

Your music, your schedule, your voice, your shows, your presenters. Sauti is a platform for you to run your station, You decide exactly what goes out to your listeners

How it works

From idea to on air in four steps.

Pick a step to see what it looks like. There’s nothing to install to listen, and nothing complicated to learn.

Add your music

Drop your songs, jingles and recorded shows into your library. Sauti reads the titles and artists, outs the volume and perform other analysis so every track sounds balanced.

  • Works with common audio files like MP3, WAV, FLAC and M4A
  • Broken or silent files are flagged, so they never reach listeners

Plan your week

Group your songs into playlists, then choose what plays and when. Gentle jazz for the morning commute, an eveining drive show, hits for Friday night, a talk show on Sunday. Whatever you desire

  • Play playlists in order or shuffled, and drop in jingles regularly
  • Set it once and Sauti follows your timetable and schedule every week

Go live whenever you like

Want to talk to your listeners? Report some news or trend? Connect a free broadcasting app on your computer, and your voice goes straight on air. When you finish, the music picks up right where the schedule left off.

  • Your Live page shows exactly what to type into the broadcasting app
  • Give each presenter their own login credentials, and cancel it any time

Share it with and to the world

Every station gets its own page with a play button, what’s playing right now and what is scheduled up next. Send the link to your listeners, or embed the player on your website. Sauti keeps the music flowing even if your internet connection wobbles.

  • No app to install and no account for listeners
  • The player reconnects by itself if the internet wobbles

What’s inside

Everything a station needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Your own music library

Upload your songs, jingles and shows and keep them tidy in one place. Sauti balances the volume so nothing blasts or whispers.

Playlists that think for you

Play in order or shuffle, and slip in a jingle every few songs. Build a playlist once and let it run.

A schedule for every day

Different sounds for mornings, evenings and weekends. Your weekly timetable runs itself, in your own time zone.

Go live in one step

Presenters connect and take over the airwaves. When they finish, your scheduled music returns automatically.

A safety net under it all

If nothing is scheduled or something goes wrong, Sauti plays from your own library, and a gentle backup loop as a last resort, so listeners don’t hear dead silence.

A page for your listeners

Each station gets a simple page with a play button and the song playing now. It works on phones, tablets and computers.
